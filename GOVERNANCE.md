# Crosscub governance

Crosscub is maintained in [tobiasweissert/Crosscub](https://github.com/tobiasweissert/Crosscub).
Owner approval controls product identity, App Store composition and stable releases.
Direct technical upstream is CrossPoint; selective CrossInk changes and historical
CrossSmudge lineage retain authorship and licenses. Upstream governance governs
upstream contributions, not Crosscub release permission.

Feature/PR builds are experimental evidence, never automatic firmware promotion.
No branch merge authorizes stable publication, Store replacement, credential policy
changes, SD/NVS reset or history rewrite. Preserve 41 Store entries, Lua saves,
Browser/AirDrop/heatmap/OTA and existing routes. See [scope/readiness](SCOPE.md).
