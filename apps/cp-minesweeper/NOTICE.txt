Minesweeper 0.1.4 — CrossPlay graphics and rules port

Rules, mine drawing and layout derived from CrossPlay by Mario Ruiz and
contributors (LICENSE-CrossPlay.txt). CrossSmudge license is retained in
LICENSE-CrossSmudge.txt.

Jersey 25 glyph pixels come from CrossPlay's generated toybox_20 and toybox_30
1-bit EpdFont cuts, repacked without resampling into row-padded Lua bitmaps.
The typeface is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1; see OFL-Jersey25.txt.

The flag comes from CrossPlay's icon_mineflag_32 (Lucide flag), resized with
nearest-neighbor sampling to the original control/cell sizes. Full Lucide
and inherited Feather notices are in LICENSE-Lucide.txt.

art/sources.json pins source-file SHA256 hashes. Reproduction tool:
test/minesweeper_graphics/assets.py in the firmware repository.
No private owner photograph, save payload, or native engine binary is included.
