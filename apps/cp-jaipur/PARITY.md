# Jaipur — original CrossPlay market presentation

On Lua heaps of at least 512 KiB, the app uses the original portrait JaipurActivity canvas: 76px black header, report line, score/deck/seals strip, five market cards, six goods piles with remaining-token pips, three bonus stacks, six hand counters, camel herd, opponent hand-count box and bottom status/commit capsule. Original Jersey 10/14/20/30 fonts and original Jaipur goods/camel/bonus masks (32/44/56) are app-private with retained licenses. Entering selects portrait; leaving restores the previous orientation. The header returns to the menu. Touch targets and physical-button focus share one control table.

Tap market cards to select; all camel cards select together. Tap a hand counter to cycle how many to sell; with market goods selected, counters instead offer goods/camels for a trade. The capsule commits only an existing-core legal move; CLEAR cancels. Tapping a goods pile/bonus stack opens the remaining-payout inspector. Opponents move automatically only on the visible board. The original eight illustrated tutorial topics, original explanatory strings, page dots and tap-to-advance are present.

The existing core, validation and checked game journal remain unchanged: six goods/token supplies, 7-card limit, two-or-more exchanges, no same-good exchange, bonus stacks, camel majority, source tie breaks, exhausted-pile/deck end, loser-first next round and two seals. Opponent private goods never appear, and hidden bonus values stay hidden until round scoring. The round ledger reports actual goods/bonus/camel aggregates: the old core does not retain each player's per-good payout history, so the original six separate goods rows cannot be recovered honestly. The report line shows whose turn it is; the original durable last-move narration is not available in the old snapshot.

Other explicit gaps: the existing public-information greedy opponent remains rather than JaipurBrain; Nearby is absent; unsupported Nearby controls are omitted. Existing completed matches before this version have no recoverable lifetime record. The original list menu's supported Continue/New Game/How To Play rows remain, with Save and Exit replacing the unavailable Nearby row. Separate bounded checked two-slot `ui-record-*.dat` files record completed matches once and recover from a torn latest slot. Five-second action autosave debounce and exit saves remain; power loss can lose recent actions. New-game replacement is confirmed while a match is active. The low-memory branch is a byte-identical copy of the previous main adapter, gated before loading artwork.

Native validation: `python3 test/hearts_jaipur_graphics/run.py` runs the exact vendored int32/float32 Lua at 75 KiB and 2 MiB. It covers complete graphical match phases, all eight tutorial pages, a real market take and exchange through drawn targets, hidden-opponent mutation checks, AI pause in menus, orientation restoration, checked save reopening, record deduplication/torn-slot/write failures, compact lifecycle and existing rule regressions. SVG captures replay actual Lua bitmap masks. Integer-rounded glyph advances, no kerning and the aggregate round ledger prevent a claim of exact pixel parity. Native allocator figures exclude renderer/HAL allocations.
