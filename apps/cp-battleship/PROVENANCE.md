# Provenance

Rules and Sticky presentation derive from CrossPlay `ma-r-s/crossplay`, pinned revision `d3a49ce8e85f9bd0d3e0c774a54adc267fd7547b`. Its Battleship and shared UI sources equal the preserved upstream parent `4d40b952f52562e3816585c98bbbaf99d269d9d1`. Copyright notices and MIT grant are in `LICENSE-CrossPlay.txt`; CrossSmudge changes use `LICENSE-CrossSmudge.txt`.

`art/font{10,20,30}.bin` are lossless one-bit ASCII Jersey 25 cuts repacked from the original `src/apps_local/ui/fonts/toybox_*.h`. `art/sources.json` pins their source hashes. Jersey's OFL notice is retained in `OFL-Jersey25.txt`. `art/nearby32.raw` derives from the original Toybox Lucide Nearby icon; its exact source hash is in `art/nearby-source.json`, with `LICENSE-Lucide.txt` retained. Neither glyphs nor icons are screenshots.

No external content bank, restricted puzzle bank or APK assets are bundled. Approved launcher icon bytes are unchanged. `legacy_{board,graphics,front}.lua` preserve the prior presentation for every non-Sticky or unidentified device.
